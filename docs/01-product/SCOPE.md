# Scope

Status convention: **CONFIRMED** / **PROPOSED** / **OPEN** / **REJECTED**. A status with no applicable item in a document is not implied.

## V1 product scope

| Status | In-scope area | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CONFIRMED | Platform administration for tenants, subscription/domain status, webhooks, logs, and settings. | SRC-001 §5.1, §14; SRC-002 §05.1, §14 |
| CONFIRMED | Tenant administration for branding, domain, staff, clients, files, tickets, Quote, PI, billing, and profile. | SRC-001 §3.2, §14; SRC-002 §03, §14 |
| CONFIRMED | Client portal for files, tickets, Quote, PI, profile, and dashboard/login surfaces. | SRC-001 §3.4, §14; SRC-002 §03, §14 |
| CONFIRMED | Tenant isolation, white label, custom domains/SSL, subscription automation, security controls, and audit logs. | SRC-001 §2, §4–§6, §12–§13; SRC-002 §02, §04–§06, §12–§13 |

## V1 exclusions

| REJECTED | Google Drive/Dropbox integrations, custom permission builder, custom email-sending domain, multilingual support, online/recurring invoice payment, knowledge base, and advanced BI. | SRC-001 §15; SRC-002 §15 |
